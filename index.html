About

I am a PhD candidate in Operations Analytics at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, working on quantitative sustainability and mathematical optimization. My research develops decision models and exact algorithms for sustainable supply chains and logistics networks, combining branch-cut-and-price, life-cycle assessment and real-world operational data.

Before joining VU, I was a research and teaching assistant in the Transportation Division at Istanbul Technical University, where I worked on electric and environment-friendly vehicle routing. I expect to defend my PhD in March 2027.
